---
title: Label switching
stub: true
lede: >-
  In a model that sorts items into unnamed groups, swapping the groups' names gives exactly the same
  likelihood. The posterior then has one mirror-image mode per relabelling, and averages taken across them
  mix the groups together. A constraint, such as ordering the groups' parameters, gives the labels their
  meaning.
notation: '\(K\) groups, \(\lambda_k\) the parameter of group \(k\), \(z_i\) the group of item \(i\).'
prerequisites: [markov-chain-monte-carlo, "wiki:Mixture model"]
further: ["wiki:Identifiability"]
